# Invoice and payment reminders

*Send the invoice on delivery, the first reminder on the due date and the second 7 days later.*

```text
Invoice [number], [date]

To: [client name, business]
From: [your name, postal address, email]

[Description of work]: [amount]
Deposit received: -[amount]
Balance due: [amount]
Due by: [date, 7 days after delivery]
How to pay: [bank transfer, PayPal or Wise details]

---

First reminder (on the due date):
Hi [name], a friendly reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] is due today. Payment details are below. Thanks!

Second reminder (7 days later):
Hi [name], invoice [number] for [amount] is now 7 days overdue. Could you let me know when to expect payment? I'll pause any further work until it's settled.

[Your name]
```
