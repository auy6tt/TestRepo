# One-page agreement

*Fill in and get a written "yes" before starting any job off-platform. Not legal advice; adapt it to your country.*

```text
Agreement between [client] and [you], [date]

Work: [specific deliverables]
Not included: [what's out of scope]
Done when: [acceptance test, e.g. 3 pages load on phone and desktop, contact button works]

Price: [amount]. 50% before starting, 50% on delivery. Payment is due within 7 days of delivery.
Revisions: 2 rounds included, then [rate] per hour.
Timeline: delivered by [date] if I receive [materials] by [date].

Ownership: once paid in full, I transfer to you all rights I hold in the final work. Third-party parts (open-source code, fonts, stock photos) stay under their own licenses. I may show the work in my portfolio unless you say no.
Cancellation: if you cancel after work starts, the deposit isn't refunded.
Tools: I use AI tools to help with the work and review everything myself. I won't share your data beyond the tools this job needs.
Accounts: domains, hosting and API keys stay in your name.
Liability: limited to the amount paid for this work.

Agreed: [client name, date]   [your name, date]
```
