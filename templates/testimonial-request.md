# Asking for a review or testimonial

*Send on delivery day, while the client is happiest.*

```text
Thanks again for working with me on [project].

If you're happy with it, would you write 2–3 sentences about what I did and how it helped? I'd like to show it on my profile with your first name and business name.

If anything isn't right, tell me first and I'll fix it.
```
