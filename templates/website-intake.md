# Website intake questions

*Send after a yes, before building the live version.*

```text
1. Business name, and what you sell in one sentence
2. Area you serve and opening hours
3. Phone and WhatsApp number to show
4. 3–6 photos you or your staff took (photos customers posted on your listing belong to them)
5. Your top 3 services, with prices if you want them shown
6. Two websites you like the look of
7. Do you already own a domain name? If yes, which?
8. Who should I contact for future updates?
```
