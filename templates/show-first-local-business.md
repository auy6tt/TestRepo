# Show-first message to a local business

*Send by hand (WhatsApp, Instagram or email) with screenshots or an unlisted demo link, after you've built their concept site, or say it in person. Check your country's marketing rules first, and stop at the first no.*

```text
Hi [owner's name], I'm [your name], and I build websites for small businesses here in [city].

[Business name] has [143] Google reviews at [4.8] stars but no website, so people searching "[service] near me" end up on other businesses' pages.

I made you a free one-page concept from your public info: [screenshots or unlisted link]. Here's a 60-second walkthrough: [video link]. It's marked as a concept, not your official site, and uses placeholder photos.

If you like it, I can put it live on your own domain this week for [price], with 2 rounds of changes included. If not, there's no charge and I'll delete it.

Worth a quick look?
[Your name], [phone number]
[Your postal address, for US emails]

Reply "no" and I won't contact you again.
```
