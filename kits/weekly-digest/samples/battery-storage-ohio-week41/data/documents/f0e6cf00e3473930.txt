Item 7: Resolution 2026-118
A resolution declaring a moratorium on the acceptance and processing of applications for zoning
permits, zoning map amendments and conditional use approvals for battery energy storage facilities in
the unincorporated areas subject to the Tarrow County Zoning Resolution, and directing the Tarrow
County Zoning Commission to study the matter and recommend amendments.
Summary prepared by the Clerk of the Board
Duration: Twelve (12) months from the effective date, unless ended earlier by resolution of the Board.
Applies to: New applications for battery energy storage facilities with a rated capacity of one (1)
megawatt or more.
Applies in: The unincorporated areas subject to the Tarrow County Zoning Resolution (currently
Ferris, Quill and Brindle townships). Townships that administer their own zoning are not affected.
Does not apply to: Applications filed and determined to be complete before the effective date; battery
systems that serve a single home or business on the same property.
Effective date: Immediately upon adoption.
Study: The Zoning Commission is directed to recommend setback, fire-safety and decommissioning
standards to the Board within nine (9) months.
Presented by the Office of the County Administrator. The full text of the resolution is available from
the Clerk of the Board.
