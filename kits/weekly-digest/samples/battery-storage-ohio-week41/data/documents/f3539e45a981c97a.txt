CITY OF MARLOWE FALLS, OHIO
ORDINANCE NO. 2026-41
As amended in the Safety Committee, October 5, 2026
AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING FIRE SAFETY AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE
REQUIREMENTS FOR BATTERY ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS.
Section 1. Applicability. This ordinance applies to battery energy storage systems with an aggregate
energy capacity greater than 600 kWh, other than systems serving a single one- or two-family
dwelling.
Section 2. Standards. Systems shall be designed, installed and maintained in accordance with NFPA
855, Standard for the Installation of Stationary Energy Storage Systems, in the edition in effect when
the permit application is filed.
Section 3. Emergency response plan. Before a certificate of occupancy is issued, the owner shall
submit an emergency response plan approved by the Fire Chief.
Section 4. Annual walkthrough. The owner shall host a site walkthrough with the Marlowe Falls Fire
Department once each calendar year.
Section 5. Signs and contacts. A 24-hour emergency contact number shall be posted at each entrance
to the site.
Section 6. Effective date. This ordinance takes effect at the earliest date allowed by law.
