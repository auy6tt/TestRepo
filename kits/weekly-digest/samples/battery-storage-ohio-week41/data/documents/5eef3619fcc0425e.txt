WRENFIELD TOWNSHIP
Tarrow County, Ohio
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
Zoning Text Amendment ZTA-2026-03: Battery Energy Storage Systems
The Wrenfield Township Zoning Commission will hold a public hearing on Wednesday, October 21,
2026, at 7:00 p.m. at the Wrenfield Township Hall, 8 Gable Road, on proposed amendments to the
Wrenfield Township Zoning Resolution.
The proposed amendments would:
1. Add definitions of "battery energy storage system" and "battery energy storage facility".
2. Allow battery energy storage facilities as a conditional use in the I-1 Light Industrial District only.
3. Require a setback of at least 300 feet from any existing dwelling and at least 100 feet from any
property line.
4. Require a decommissioning plan and a financial guarantee (bond or letter of credit) covering
removal costs, to be reviewed every five years.
5. Require a fire-safety plan reviewed by the Fire Chief before a zoning certificate is issued.
The proposed text may be inspected at the Township Hall during office hours and on the township
website. Written comments may be submitted to the Zoning Inspector until 4:00 p.m. on Friday,
October 16, 2026. After the hearing, the Zoning Commission will send its recommendation to the
Board of Township Trustees.
By order of the Wrenfield Township Zoning Commission. Posted October 5, 2026.
