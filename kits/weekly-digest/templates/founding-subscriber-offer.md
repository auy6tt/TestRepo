# "Founding subscriber" offer

Use this to sign up your first 5 to 10 paying subscribers before you have any reviews. There are three parts: a short message, a longer email or landing page, and the terms. Replace everything in [square brackets]. Suggested prices for each offer are in the README.

Send the sample issue with every message: the PDF from `samples/` for a first contact, or a real issue for your niche once you have one.

## Short version (LinkedIn message, email or forum reply)

> Hi [name], I'm starting [Digest name], a weekly brief on [topic] in [region] for [audience]. Every [Monday]: what changed at [number] official sources, why it matters and the dates you need, with each item checked against the source. Here's a sample issue: [link or attachment].
>
> I'm taking [10] founding subscribers at [$X] a month (it will be [$Y]), locked in for 12 months. Want me to send you next week's issue free so you can judge it?

## Longer version (email or landing page)

**[Digest name]: [one-line promise, for example "every battery storage zoning change in Ohio, every Monday"]**

[Two sentences on the problem, for example: "Rezonings, moratoria and hearing dates for battery storage now change every week across dozens of counties and townships. Most of it is buried in agenda PDFs that nobody has time to read."]

Every [Monday] you get:

- every relevant new item from [number] official sources, in plain English, with a link to the document and the page
- a "coming up" list of hearings, votes and deadlines, plus a calendar file
- a spreadsheet tracker you can filter and share with your team
- a PDF to forward or print

A person checks every item against its source before it is sent. It summarises public documents and is not legal advice.

**Founding subscriber offer, for the first [10] only:**

- [$X] a month instead of [$Y], locked for 12 months
- your first [2] issues free: cancel before the third and you pay nothing
- ask for up to [3] extra [counties, agencies or states] to be added
- a 15-minute call after week 4 to tell me what to change

[Button or link: Start with a free issue]

## Terms (put these on your sign-up page)

- **Price:** [$X] a month, billed monthly from your third issue. The founding price stays for 12 months while you remain subscribed.
- **Cancel any time:** reply "cancel" or use [link]. No further charges. [Refund for the current month if you ask within 7 days.]
- **What it is:** summaries of public documents with links to the official sources. Not legal, tax or financial advice. Dates can change after we send, so confirm with the source before you act.
- **Sharing:** for use inside your organisation. Please do not republish or resell it.
- **Corrections:** if we find an error, we correct it in the next issue and email you sooner if a date changes.
- **Privacy:** we use your email only to send the brief and messages about your subscription. [Link to your privacy note.]

## Notes for you (do not send)

- Ask for feedback, not reviews. Never offer a discount, gift or free month in return for a positive review or testimonial.
- Keep the founding group small so you can talk to each person.
- After 4 weeks, ask each subscriber: "What would make this worth [$Y] to you?" Raise the price for new subscribers only.
- Keep your subscriber list in your email or payment tool, not in this repository.
