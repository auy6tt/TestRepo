# Subscriber welcome email

Send this on the day someone subscribes, or set it as the automatic welcome email in your newsletter tool. Replace everything in [square brackets]. Keep it short: people read the first three lines.

---

**Subject:** Welcome to [Digest name]: your first issue arrives [Monday, October 12]

Hi [first name],

Thanks for subscribing to [Digest name]. Here is what to expect.

**When it arrives.** Every [Monday] by [noon Eastern]. If a week is quiet, you still get a short note saying we checked every source and found nothing new, so you never have to wonder.

**What is in each issue.**

- A short email with every new item: what changed, why it matters and a link to the official document.
- A PDF of the same issue, for printing or forwarding to your team.
- A spreadsheet tracker with one row per item and columns for your own status and notes.
- A calendar file (.ics) with every hearing, vote and deadline. Open it on your computer to add the dates to Outlook or Apple Calendar; in Google Calendar, use Settings, then Import & export, then Import. Each date comes with a reminder the day before.

**What we watch.** [Number] official sources: [for example, county commission agendas, planning commission packets and township legal notices across 14 Ohio counties]. If you want a [county, agency or state] added, reply and tell me.

**How we check.** A person reads every item against its source document before it goes out. We link to the official documents instead of copying them, so you can always see the original.

**What it is not.** [Digest name] summarises public documents. It is not legal advice and does not tell you what to do. Before you act on a date or a rule, confirm it with the source or your advisers.

**Corrections.** If we get something wrong, we correct it at the top of the next issue, and we email you sooner if a date changes.

**Your subscription.** [$X] a month, billed through [Stripe, Gumroad or your tool]. [Founding subscribers keep this price for 12 months.] Cancel any time by replying "cancel" or at [link]; there is nothing else to do.

Your first issue arrives on [date]. Reply to this email with any question: it comes straight to me.

[Your name]
[Brand] · [postal address]
