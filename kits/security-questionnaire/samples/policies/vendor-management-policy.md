# Vendor Management Policy

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE. Quarterhour, Inc. is a made-up company used to demonstrate this kit.

- Document ID: QH-POL-09
- Version: 1.0
- Owner: Dana Whitfield, CTO
- Approved by: Maya Okafor, CEO
- Last reviewed: 20 May 2026
- Review cycle: at least annually

## 1. Purpose

To manage the security risk of third parties that store or process Quarterhour or customer data.

## 2. Vendor register

Quarterhour keeps a register of vendors that store or process company or customer data. For each vendor it records the data involved, the risk level and the contract owner. Vendors that process customer personal data are also listed as subprocessors on the public subprocessor list.

## 3. Assessment before use

Before a new vendor receives customer data:

- the contract owner completes a vendor risk review;
- for high-risk vendors (those that store or process customer data), Quarterhour reviews the vendor's SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certificate or completed security questionnaire;
- a data processing agreement is signed if the vendor will process personal data.

The CTO approves every high-risk vendor.

## 4. Contracts

Vendor contracts must include confidentiality obligations and security requirements, plus data protection terms where personal data is involved.

## 5. Ongoing review

High-risk vendors are reviewed every year, including their latest security report. Other vendors are reviewed when their contract renews.

## 6. Subprocessor changes

Customers are told at least 30 days before a new subprocessor starts processing their personal data. Notice is sent by email to customer account administrators and the public subprocessor list is updated.

## 7. Key vendors

- Amazon Web Services: hosting, database and backups (high risk).
- Google Workspace: company email and documents (high risk).
- GitHub: source code hosting (medium risk).
- Stripe: payment processing (medium risk). Card details are entered on Stripe-hosted payment forms. Quarterhour systems do not store or process full card numbers.
- Transactional email and SMS providers: appointment reminders (high risk).

## 8. Ending a vendor relationship

When a vendor is no longer used, its access is removed and it is asked to confirm deletion or return of our data.
