# Questions for Quarterhour, Inc. (fictional)

Your documents don't cover these topics. A short factual answer for each is enough. If something is not in place, just say so: an honest "No" or "Not yet" is fine. If a document covers it, send the document instead.

## Governance & Policies

- [ ] **GOV-004** Does the company carry out a formal security risk assessment at least once a year?
- [ ] **GOV-005** Do employees and contractors formally acknowledge the security policies?

## Compliance & Certifications

- [ ] **CMP-003** How does the company meet GDPR requirements when it processes personal data of people in the EU or UK?
- [ ] **CMP-005** Does the company carry cyber liability insurance?

## People Security

- [ ] **HR-005** Is there a disciplinary process for breaches of security policy?

## Access Control

- [ ] **AC-005** Does the product support single sign-on (SAML or OIDC) for customer users?

## Encryption & Keys

- [ ] **CRY-006** Can customers manage their own encryption keys?

## Data Protection & Privacy

- [ ] **DAT-001** What types of customer data does the service store?
- [ ] **DAT-008** How does the company handle requests from individuals to access or delete their personal data?
- [ ] **DAT-009** Does the company classify data by sensitivity?
- [ ] **DAT-010** Does the company publish a privacy policy that covers the service?

## Business Continuity & DR

- [ ] **BCP-006** What uptime commitment (SLA) does the company offer?

## Incident Response

- [ ] **IR-007** Has the company had a security breach affecting customer data in the past three years?

## Vulnerability Management

- [ ] **VUL-002** Are systems and infrastructure scanned for vulnerabilities regularly?
- [ ] **VUL-004** How are operating systems and infrastructure patched?

## Logging & Monitoring

- [ ] **LOG-002** How long are security logs kept?
- [ ] **LOG-004** Are logs protected from tampering and deletion?
- [ ] **LOG-005** Does the product give customers an audit log of user activity?

## Network & Infrastructure

- [ ] **NET-002** How is network access to production systems restricted?
- [ ] **NET-004** Is the application protected by a web application firewall and DDoS protection?
- [ ] **NET-005** Are servers and cloud resources configured to a hardened baseline?

## Endpoints & Assets

- [ ] **END-002** Is anti-malware or endpoint detection software installed on company devices?
- [ ] **END-003** Does the company keep an inventory of hardware and software assets?
- [ ] **END-004** Can staff use personal devices to access company or customer data?
- [ ] **END-005** Are screen locks and automatic security updates enforced on company devices?
