# Cover note for a delivered questionnaire

*Send part A with the draft and part B with the final version. Fill in the [brackets]. The counts are on line 2 of the Review sheet.*

## A. Draft for the client's review

```text
Subject: [Buyer] security questionnaire: draft ready for your review

Hi [name],

Attached is the draft of [Buyer]'s [questionnaire name] ([N] questions), due to them on [date].

What's in it
- [N] answers drafted from your own documents. Each one names its source (document and section) in the Evidence column and on the "Review" sheet.
- [N] questions marked NEEDS CLIENT INPUT. Your documents don't cover them; they're listed at the top of the Review sheet.
- [N] answers marked CHECK. Please confirm these: the match was weaker, or the question asks for something specific such as a date or an attachment.

What I need from you by [date]
1. A short answer to each NEEDS CLIENT INPUT question. "No" or "not yet" is a fine answer; please don't guess.
2. A yes or a correction for each CHECK item.
3. [Technical owner] to read every answer and approve it. These are statements your company makes to [Buyer], so they must be true today.

Good to know
- I used only the documents you sent and your written answers. I haven't added controls, dates or certifications you don't have.
- This is not an audit or a certification. It's your own answers, organised and sourced.
- Your files are handled under our NDA and deleted on [date] [or: kept in your client folder while we're on retainer].

Thanks,
[Your name]
```
