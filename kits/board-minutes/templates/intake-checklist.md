# Intake checklist: board meeting minutes

Use the first three parts once per new client. Use "Every meeting" each time a recording comes in.

## 1. Before you say yes

- [ ] It's a meeting type you handle: a board, committee or annual members' meeting.
- [ ] Turnaround agreed: 24, 48 or 72 hours from when you receive the transcript and agenda.
- [ ] Price agreed (per meeting or monthly) and how you get paid. Ask new clients for a deposit, or the first meeting paid upfront.
- [ ] A short written agreement is signed. It covers confidentiality, AI-assisted drafting, deletion of files, and a liability cap at the fee, and says the board approves the minutes. A starting point is [`templates/simple-agreement.md`](../../../templates/simple-agreement.md) at the root of this repo.
- [ ] The client knows you draft with AI tools and check every line yourself. If they say "no AI", don't use this kit for them.
- [ ] Model training is turned off in your Claude privacy settings before you touch any client file.

## 2. Set up once per association

- [ ] **Board roster:** exact names and titles, plus committee chairs. Spellings come from here, not from the transcript.
- [ ] **Quorum rule:** for example "a majority of the board: 3 of 5 directors".
- [ ] **Template:** their own minutes template (.docx), or their OK to use yours.
- [ ] **Two or three past approved minutes**, so you can match their style and level of detail.
- [ ] **Style:** decisions only ("action minutes"), or decisions plus short discussion summaries?
- [ ] **Votes:** record counts? Names on roll calls? Name abstentions?
- [ ] **Open forum:** name residents who speak, or not? (Default in this kit: not named.)
- [ ] **Executive session:** what may the minutes say? (Default: the time, the general topic, and any vote taken afterwards in open session.)
- [ ] **Who gets the draft** (secretary, manager or president) and who answers your questions.
- [ ] **Format:** US or UK dates and paper size, PDF as well as Word, file naming.
- [ ] **Delivery:** email, shared drive or management portal.
- [ ] **Retention:** how long you may keep recordings and transcripts, and how you'll delete them.

## 3. Recording consent

- [ ] Ask how attendees are told the meeting is recorded. Most boards announce it at the start; check that the transcript shows this.
- [ ] If there's no announcement, ask the client to confirm the recording was lawful where they are before you start. Some US states and many countries require everyone's consent to record.
- [ ] Don't record meetings yourself unless the client arranges it and attendees are told.

## 4. Every meeting

- [ ] **Transcript file** from the meeting software, not only the audio:
  - Zoom cloud recording: the audio transcript file (.vtt)
  - Microsoft Teams: the meeting transcript, downloaded as .vtt or .docx
  - Otter and similar tools: export as .txt, .docx or .srt
- [ ] **Agenda**, and the board packet if motions refer to documents in it.
- [ ] **The recording**, if you may keep it until the minutes are approved. It helps you check unclear parts.
- [ ] **Attendance list** from the meeting software (optional; helps with names).
- [ ] **Secretary's notes**: late arrivals, early departures, recusals, anything said off the recording.
- [ ] **Deadline** for this draft, and anything unusual about the meeting (special meeting, election, closed session).

## Message to a new client (copy and edit)

```text
Hi [Name],

Thanks for choosing me for your board minutes. To get started I need:

1. The board list with each person's title (I use it for exact spellings)
2. Your quorum rule
3. Your minutes template, if you have one, and two recent approved minutes
4. For each meeting: the transcript file from Zoom, Teams or Otter, plus the agenda

A few quick choices:
- Decisions only, or decisions plus short discussion summaries?
- Should residents who speak in the open forum be named? (I leave them unnamed unless you say otherwise.)
- What may the minutes say about executive session? (Usually just the time and general topic.)

Turnaround is [48] hours from when I have the transcript and agenda. I draft with
AI-assisted tools and check every name, motion and vote myself. Your files stay
confidential, and I delete recordings and transcripts once the board approves the
minutes.

Thanks,
[Your name]
```
