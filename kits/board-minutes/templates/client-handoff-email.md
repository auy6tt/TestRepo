# Client handoff emails

Copy, fill in the brackets, and paste your questions from `check_minutes.py --questions-out`. Send files the way the client prefers (their portal or shared drive is better than an open email attachment).

## 1. Draft delivery

```text
Subject: Draft minutes: [Association], [regular] board meeting, [Month Day, Year]

Hi [Name],

Attached are the draft minutes for the [Month Day] [regular] board meeting:

- [name]-minutes.docx: the draft minutes in Word, ready for your edits
- [name]-minutes.pdf: the same draft as a PDF
- [name]-motions.xlsx: motions and votes, action items, and the questions below

In short: [8] motions, [all carried]; [9] action items.

I need your help with [3] points. Each is highlighted in yellow in the draft:

1. [Question 1]
2. [Question 2]
3. [Question 3]

Please reply with answers and any corrections by [day]. I'll send the final
draft within [24 hours] of your reply. The minutes stay a draft until the board
approves them.

As agreed, I prepared this draft with AI-assisted tools and checked every name,
motion and vote against the meeting transcript myself. I'll delete the recording
and transcript [once the board approves the minutes / on date] unless you'd like
them deleted sooner.

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

## 2. Final draft after the secretary's answers

```text
Subject: Final draft minutes: [Association], [Month Day, Year]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the answers. The attached final draft includes them:

- [Change 1, for example: Raymond Castillo recorded as seconder of Motion 2]
- [Change 2]

It's ready for the board's approval at the [Month Day] meeting. After the board
approves it, send me any corrections made at the meeting and I'll mark the
minutes as approved.

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

## 3. Approved version

```text
Subject: Approved minutes: [Association], [Month Day, Year]

Hi [Name],

Attached are the minutes as approved by the board on [approval date], with the
draft label removed. As agreed, I've now deleted the recording and transcript
for this meeting.

Thanks,
[Your name]
```

## Before you press send

- The questions are specific and point to a place in the recording, so the secretary can answer in a minute.
- Nothing from executive session is in the email beyond what the minutes say.
- No resident's personal details are in the email.
- The attachments are this client's files, for this meeting, in their final versions.
